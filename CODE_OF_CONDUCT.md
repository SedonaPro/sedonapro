# Community guidelines

Be welcoming, patient and specific. People arrive with different languages,
engineering backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Critique ideas and code respectfully. Explain disagreements with evidence.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten or publish anyone's private information.
- Keep discussions relevant. Avoid spam and undisclosed promotion.
- Respect other people's work, licences, equipment and time.

These guidelines apply to issues, pull requests, Discussions and other spaces
managed by this organization. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock a
conversation, or restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions
should be proportionate to the behaviour and its effect on others.

Use GitHub's reporting controls for abusive content, or contact an organization
owner privately. Do not repost private reports in public discussions.
